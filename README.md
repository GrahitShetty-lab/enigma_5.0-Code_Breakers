Problem Statement: When someone dies, their family doesn't just grieve — they inherit a nightmare: forgotten insurance policies, unclaimed EPF/PPF accounts, active loan EMIs still getting deducted, subscriptions auto-renewing, and nominee details that are missing or outdated, with no single record of what the person even had. Banks, insurers and digital platforms all run separate, painful, paperwork-heavy death-claim processes. As a result, thousands of crores sit unclaimed in Indian financial institutions every year simply because families never find out an account existed.
